Working with children—both in holiday camps and in care homes—has made me acutely aware of the **educational and social inequalities** they can face from an early age. This motivates me to take action at my own level to help bridge that gap.

I have strong **interpersonal skills**, solid scientific foundations, and teaching experience that I want to put to good use for a meaningful cause. **Children are a priority for me**: they are the citizens of tomorrow. Being able to contribute, even in a small way, to their confidence and academic success is a source of **great personal satisfaction**.
