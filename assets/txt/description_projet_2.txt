This **C++** multi-agent simulation models competing **ant colonies** foraging for sugar, laying down **pheromone trails**, reproducing, and defending their nests until only one colony survives[cite: 3].

Built with **SFML 2.6** and compiled via a custom **Makefile**, the application offers three output modes: a lightweight **terminal view**, an **interactive graphical simulation**, and an automated **animated GIF generator** (`gifMaker`) to visualise colony strategies over time[cite: 3].
