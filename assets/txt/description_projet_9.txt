Written in **C** with **SDL2**, this application computes and animates **Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm** across 2D elevation terrains where edge costs account for both Euclidean distance and positive altitude climbs[cite: 9].

The software separates the **SDL2 rendering loop** from the **pathfinding computation thread** using mutexes and semaphores, providing real-time step-by-step animations of visited nodes, obstacles, and optimal path reconstruction, validated with unit tests and **Valgrind**[cite: 9].
