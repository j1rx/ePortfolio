I have always loved **cooking**, a passion passed down to me by my **grandmother**. I have always enjoyed cooking for others, seeing the smiles on their faces, organising myself, and taking care of every detail.

I love imagining and creating **new recipes**. Cooking is a real refuge for me, a moment of **calm and creativity**. I often considered making it my profession, but in the end I chose to keep this passion as a **personal pleasure**, without turning it into a chore.
