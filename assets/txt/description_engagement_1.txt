As a **summer camp counselor** for three years, I have enjoyed interacting with people and managing group dynamics. I have supervised a variety of trips—including skiing, sailing, surfing, and touring Italy—learning to strike the right balance between **authority and kindness**. This demanding role has allowed me to develop my **creativity, patience, and resilience**, while helping me grow both personally and professionally.

During these camps, our mission is also to raise children's awareness of important topics, particularly **ecology**, through clean walks and workshops on eco-friendly everyday habits. I have also had the opportunity to work with **children with disabilities** on several occasions—a deeply human experience that strengthened my **listening skills, patience, and adaptability**.
