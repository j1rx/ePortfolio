Instead of going on a solidarity trip in my first year, I worked as a **holiday camp counselor** during two summer camps, including a **three-week itinerant camp** across **Italy and Slovenia** with CIE Thales.

Supervising a group of teenagers abroad while communicating **daily in English** with local partners and guides was a real challenge. This first stage of my mobility taught me how to **adapt quickly** to unfamiliar environments, manage logistics on the move, and collaborate effectively in an **international context**.
